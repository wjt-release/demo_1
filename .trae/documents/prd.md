# Product Requirements Document (PRD): Aura - Modern Women's Fashion E-commerce

## 1. Introduction
**Product Name:** Aura
**Description:** A minimalist, modern women's fashion e-commerce website tailored for Chinese consumers. Inspired by the aesthetics of Zara and H&M, Aura offers a seamless shopping experience with a focus on visual storytelling and user-centric design.

## 2. Goals & Objectives
- **Primary Goal:** Create a visually stunning and highly functional e-commerce platform that drives sales and brand loyalty.
- **Secondary Goal:** Establish a distinct brand identity through minimalist design and high-quality imagery.
- **Target Audience:** Fashion-forward women in China who value style, quality, and modern aesthetics.

## 3. User Personas
- **The Trendsetter:** Always looking for the latest styles. Values high-quality images and curated collections.
- **The Practical Shopper:** Needs clear product information (sizes, materials) and a smooth checkout process.
- **The Mobile User:** Shops primarily on her phone. Expects a responsive and fast mobile experience.

## 4. Key Features

### 4.1 Homepage
- **Hero Section:** Full-screen carousel showcasing latest collections and promotions.
- **New Arrivals:** Curated list of new products.
- **Best Sellers:** Highlight popular items.
- **New User Popup:** A modal offering a discount or welcome message for first-time visitors.

### 4.2 User Authentication
- **Sign Up/Login:** Email-based registration and login.
- **Forgot Password:** (Optional/Future) Email recovery flow.
- **Account Dashboard:** Overview of user profile and order history.

### 4.3 Product Catalog
- **Product List Page:** Grid layout with filtering (Category, Price, Size) and sorting options.
- **Product Detail Page:**
    - High-resolution image gallery with zoom functionality.
    - Detailed product description, material info, and care instructions.
    - Size selection and size guide.
    - User reviews and ratings.
    - "Add to Cart" and "Buy Now" buttons.

### 4.4 Shopping Cart & Checkout
- **Cart Page:** View items, update quantities, remove items. Summary of costs.
- **Checkout Process:**
    - Step 1: Shipping Address Input.
    - Step 2: Payment Method Selection (Mock Payment).
    - Step 3: Order Review and Confirmation.
- **Payment Integration:** Mock payment gateway where clicking "Pay" simulates a successful transaction.

### 4.5 Order Management
- **Order History:** List of past orders with status (Processing, Shipped, Delivered).
- **Order Details:** Detailed view of a specific order (items, total, shipping address).

### 4.6 Contact & Support
- **Contact Page:** Simple form or email link (`support@aura.com`) for customer inquiries.

### 4.7 "Coming Soon" State
- **Placeholder Pages:** Any page not yet implemented (e.g., Blog, FAQ) should display a stylish "Coming Soon" message.

## 5. Visual Design & UX
- **Style:** Minimalist, clean, and sophisticated.
- **Color Palette:**
    - **Primary:** Black (`#000000`), White (`#FFFFFF`), Gray (`#F5F5F5`).
    - **Accents:** Beige (`#F5F5DC`) or Dark Blue (`#00008B`) for buttons/links.
- **Typography:** Modern sans-serif fonts (e.g., Inter, Roboto, or similar) for readability and elegance.
- **Imagery:** Large, high-quality editorial-style photos.
- **Icons:** Unified, consistent icon set (Lucide React or similar). **NO Emojis.**
- **Animations:** Subtle, smooth transitions (fade-ins, slide-ups) to enhance the premium feel.
- **Responsiveness:** Fully optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop.

## 6. Technical Constraints
- **Platform:** Web (Responsive).
- **Browser Support:** Modern browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge).
- **Performance:** Fast load times (< 2s for LCP).

## 7. Success Metrics
- **Conversion Rate:** % of visitors who make a purchase.
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** Average spend per order.
- **Cart Abandonment Rate:** % of users who add items but don't checkout.
- **User Retention:** % of returning customers.
